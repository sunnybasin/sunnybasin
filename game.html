game

note catcher: music notes drop on the beat. click or hold them before they hit the floor.

  • ♪ notes: click once to catch them (1 point)
  • numbered notes: press and hold for that many beats until the ring fills, then they vanish. 2 beats is 3 points, 4 beats is 6 points
  • let go early and the ring resets. a note you're holding stops falling
  • the floor pulses and ticks on each beat. click right on the beat for +1 bonus
  • missing a note costs a heart, and you start with 3
  • the tempo and the fall speed keep climbing, so it gets faster and faster
  • your best score is saved in this browser
